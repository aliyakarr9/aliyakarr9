### 📋 How I work on a product

For Pandu I keep three documents next to the code (in Turkish):

- **[Product requirements](https://github.com/aliyakarr9/Pandu/blob/main/docs/PRD.md):** the problem, who it's for, what's in and out of scope, success metrics and open risks.
- **[Decision log](https://github.com/aliyakarr9/Pandu/blob/main/docs/KARARLAR.md):** twelve product decisions, each with the reason behind it and what it cost.
- **[Roadmap](https://github.com/aliyakarr9/Pandu/blob/main/docs/YOL-HARITASI.md):** what comes next, in priority order, and the question each item answers.

The other projects will get the same treatment.

### 🧭 What I care about

- **Starting from the problem.** I try to write down who something is for and how I'd know it works before adding features.
- **Writing down trade-offs.** Every decision costs something, and I'd like the cost to be on record next to the reason.
- **Apps that work without an internet connection.** Your data stays on your device unless you choose otherwise.
- **Details people feel.** I treat haptics, Reduce Motion support, VoiceOver labels and Dark Mode as part of the feature.
